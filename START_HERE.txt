SMART IRRIGATION SYSTEM - ESP32 SENSOR FRONT-END

1. Read README.md, then docs/GETTING_STARTED.md.
2. Firmware source: src/main.cpp and lib/IrrigationCore/.
3. Wiring diagrams: images/ and hardware/WIRING.md.
4. Technical report: reports/Smart_Irrigation_ESP32_Technical_Report.pdf.
5. Dashboard preview: open web/dashboard.html in your browser (synthetic data).
6. Calibrate your actual sensor before arming; no usable endpoints are assumed.
7. Physical pump output is disabled in the default build. The LED shows requests.
8. Real photographs and hardware measurements must be added from your prototype.
9. Publishing steps: docs/GITHUB_UPLOAD.md.
